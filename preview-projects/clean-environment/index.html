Keeping Our Environment Clean

Together We Can Create a Cleaner and Healthier Environment

About the Project

This project educates people about keeping our environment clean and healthy.

The Problem

Environmental pollution and poor waste disposal are making our surroundings dirty and unhealthy.

Causes of the Problem

  • Littering
  • Poor waste disposal
  • Deforestation
  • Plastic pollution
  • Air pollution
  • Lack of awareness

Solutions

  • Community cleaning
  • Use dustbins
  • Recycle plastics
  • Plant trees
  • Environmental education

Our Goal

🌱 ♻️

Encourage everyone to protect the environment for future generations.
